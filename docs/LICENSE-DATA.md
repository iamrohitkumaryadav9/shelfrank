## Other sources

- ESCI (Amazon Shopping Queries Dataset) is Apache-2.0. The class and slot students were trained on 2,000 of its queries, and the
  suggestion corpus is 1,600 of them.
- bge-small-en-v1.5 (BAAI) is MIT. The demo downloads its ONNX file from Hugging Face at a pinned revision, checked by SHA-256.
